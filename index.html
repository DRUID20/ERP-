About Us

Uganda's Newest
Oil Marketing Company

Awdeer Energy Limited is a Ugandan Oil Marketing Company (OMC) that commenced operations in January 2026. Our core business is the wholesale supply of Petrol (PMS) and Diesel (AGO) to fuel stations, bulk consumers, and institutional clients across Uganda.

Built on the principles of reliability, safety, and professional fuel distribution, we are committed to becoming a cornerstone of Uganda's energy supply chain. Our focus on compliance, operational excellence, and long-term partnerships sets us apart in the market.

Compliance & Standards Full compliance with Ugandan regulatory requirements
